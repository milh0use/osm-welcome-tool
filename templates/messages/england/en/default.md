I saw that you made your first edit on OpenStreetMap. Congratulations and thank you! You're now a contributor to OpenStreetMap.

**OpenStreetMap United Kingdom** OpenStreetMap UK (OSMUK) is a community and non-profit organisation passionate about OpenStreetMap. We represent all mappers in the United Kingdom, including Northern Ireland, the Isle of Man and Channel Islands as well as the interests of the map itself. We hope to share this with you and to help you to become part of the OpenStreetMap family. 

If you've got any questions, we can help you: if you don't know for sure how to map something, or just want to know more about OpenStreetMap, you can contact us as follows:

- **Website** Visit us at [osmuk.org](https://openstreetmap.ie)
- **Mailing list** Sign up for the mailing list [talk-gb@openstreetmap](https://lists.openstreetmap.org/listinfo/talk-gb). Note that there is also an Ireland forum that covers the whole island of Ireland at [talk-ie@openstreetmap](https://lists.openstreetmap.org/listinfo/talk-ie) 
- **Forum** United Kingdom sub-forum in the [OpenStreetMap Forums](https://community.openstreetmap.org/c/communities/uk/86)
- **Meetups** There are regional groups listed on the UK section of the [OSM Wiki](https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/United_Kingdom#Community) which arrange in-person meetups. These meetups are listed in the [OSM Calendar](https://osmcal.org/) too.
